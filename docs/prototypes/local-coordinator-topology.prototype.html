Migmate local coordinator topology

The question this answers: what process and ownership topology should connect the shared engine, the CLI, the local web UI, durable jobs, and the Migmate-managed transfer worker (rclone), so that exactly one thing ever writes a job while anyone may safely watch it? Pick a topology below, then push it through the awkward cases: someone else already running the job, a stop halfway, a laptop that loses power mid-run, two jobs at once, a closed browser tab, and another program on the same machine poking the transfer worker.

Prototype for Choose the local coordinator topology.
