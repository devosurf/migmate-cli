# Teams → Google Chat migration research

This note captures the primary-source facts that matter when deciding whether Microsoft Teams content can be **recreated** in Google Chat, must be **archived** instead, or will **lose fidelity**. The repository had no existing notes/docs convention, so this was saved under `docs/research/`.

## Executive takeaways

- Microsoft’s native export surfaces cover Teams chats and channel messages, with separate paths for chat export, channel export, retained messages, and Purview eDiscovery. Exported data includes attachments, reactions, thread replies, and message timestamps; compliance storage differs by content type. [Microsoft Teams Export APIs](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/export-teams-content), [chat: getAllMessages](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/chats-getallmessages?view=graph-rest-1.0), [channel: getAllMessages](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/graph/api/channel-getallmessages?view=graph-rest-1.0), [Teams eDiscovery locations](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/edisc-search-teams)
- Google Chat import mode is **not** a DM import target. It supports `SPACE` and `GROUP_CHAT`, not `DIRECT_MESSAGE`, and it imports messages, reactions, attachments, memberships, and historical timestamps into a temporary import-mode space that must be completed within 90 days. [Import data to Google Chat](https://developers.google.com/workspace/chat/import-data), [Authorize Google Chat apps to import data](https://developers.google.com/workspace/chat/authorize-import), [REST resource: spaces](https://developers.google.com/workspace/chat/api/reference/rest/v1/spaces)
- Google Vault is a compliance layer for Chat retention, holds, search, and export; Chat archiving is a separate first-party archive-forwarding mechanism that logs Chat activity to an email address. Neither is a migration destination. [Use Vault to search Google Chat](https://support.google.com/vault/answer/7654076?hl=en), [Retain Google Chat messages with Vault](https://support.google.com/vault/answer/7657597?hl=en), [Supported services & data types](https://support.google.com/vault/answer/6127699?hl=en), [Archive Chat messages](https://developers.google.com/workspace/chat/chat-archiving)
- Among commercial vendors, CloudM, Cloudiway, and AvePoint all document Teams → Google Chat/Spaces migration claims, but their scopes differ materially. CloudM documents channel → Space migration and says 1:1 chats are email-only; Cloudiway documents Teams channels and chats to Google Spaces, including 1:1/group chats; AvePoint publicly lists Teams → Google Chat Space and Teams Chat → Google Chat Space support paths, but the detailed support tables live in separate support-list pages. [CloudM guide](https://support.cloudm.io/hc/en-us/articles/15886942950556-Migrating-Microsoft-Teams-to-Google-Workspace-Spaces), [CloudM watchpoints](https://support.cloudm.io/hc/en-us/articles/16345799645852-Microsoft-365-to-Google-Workspace-Migration-Watchpoints), [Cloudiway guide](https://cloudiway.com/documentation/microsoft-teams-to-google-spaces-migration/), [AvePoint Fly](https://learn.avepoint.com/fly-user-guide/about-fly.html), [AvePoint support lists](https://learn.avepoint.com/fly-support-lists/about-support-lists.html)

## Quick classification

- **Recreate in Google Chat:** Teams channels, group chats, and some meeting-chat history can be represented as Google Chat spaces or group conversations; CloudM and Cloudiway both document channel/chat migration into Spaces. [CloudM guide](https://support.cloudm.io/hc/en-us/articles/15886942950556-Migrating-Microsoft-Teams-to-Google-Workspace-Spaces), [Cloudiway guide](https://cloudiway.com/documentation/microsoft-teams-to-google-spaces-migration/), [Import data](https://developers.google.com/workspace/chat/import-data)
- **Archive only:** meeting recordings, tabs, connectors, apps/bots, Planner, and Wiki are either explicitly excluded by Cloudiway or not documented as Chat equivalents, so they should be treated as file/archive work rather than Chat reconstruction. [Cloudiway guide](https://cloudiway.com/documentation/microsoft-teams-to-google-spaces-migration/), [Export Teams content](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoftteams/export-teams-content)
- **Fidelity loss:** Google import mode has no native `DIRECT_MESSAGE` target, Google Chat does not surface historical membership `createTime` in Vault/Admin console, and CloudM documents 1:1 chats as email-only. [Import data](https://developers.google.com/workspace/chat/import-data), [REST resource: spaces](https://developers.google.com/workspace/chat/api/reference/rest/v1/spaces), [CloudM watchpoints](https://support.cloudm.io/hc/en-us/articles/16345799645852-Microsoft-365-to-Google-Workspace-Migration-Watchpoints)

## Vendor claims verified from first-party docs

### CloudM

- CloudM’s current Teams → Google Workspace Spaces guide says it migrates public channel messages, public channel attachments, private and shared channel messages, private and shared channel attachments, message created date/time, reactions, channel members, and inline images. [CloudM guide](https://support.cloudm.io/hc/en-us/articles/15886942950556-Migrating-Microsoft-Teams-to-Google-Workspace-Spaces)
- CloudM’s watchpoints say 1:1 chats are migrated as **Email only**, and attachments from a local PC in 1:1 chats are not migrated. [CloudM watchpoints](https://support.cloudm.io/hc/en-us/articles/16345799645852-Microsoft-365-to-Google-Workspace-Migration-Watchpoints)
- CloudM says attachments are linked through document mappings and transferred to a Google Shared Drive named after the destination Space; if the mapping is missing, the attachment is skipped. [CloudM guide](https://support.cloudm.io/hc/en-us/articles/15886942950556-Migrating-Microsoft-Teams-to-Google-Workspace-Spaces)

### Cloudiway

- Cloudiway says it migrates Microsoft Teams channels and chats to Google Chat Spaces, using Microsoft Graph to read and Google Chat API to write. [Cloudiway guide](https://cloudiway.com/documentation/microsoft-teams-to-google-spaces-migration/)
- Cloudiway’s public guide says the migration includes channel messages, private chats (1:1 and group chats), files, attachments, reactions, mentions, and member information, and it maps 1:1 chats to direct messages or group conversations. [Cloudiway guide](https://cloudiway.com/documentation/microsoft-teams-to-google-spaces-migration/)
- Cloudiway explicitly says Teams meeting recordings are **not** migrated as part of the Teams → Spaces migration and must be handled separately. [Cloudiway guide](https://cloudiway.com/documentation/microsoft-teams-to-google-spaces-migration/)
- Cloudiway’s public guide also documents delta passes: only new messages, files/attachments, and members are transferred on later passes. [Cloudiway guide](https://cloudiway.com/documentation/microsoft-teams-to-google-spaces-migration/)

### AvePoint

- AvePoint’s public About Fly page lists **Microsoft Teams to Google Chat Space** and **Microsoft Teams Chat to Google Chat Space** as supported migration paths. [About Fly](https://learn.avepoint.com/fly-user-guide/about-fly.html)
- AvePoint’s support-list index shows that those are separate support-list documents under Microsoft Teams Migration and Microsoft Teams Chat Migration. [About Support Lists](https://learn.avepoint.com/fly-support-lists/about-support-lists.html)
- The public About Fly page is less specific than CloudM/Cloudiway about fidelity details, so treat AvePoint’s public claim as “path supported” rather than “all content elements documented.” [About Fly](https://learn.avepoint.com/fly-user-guide/about-fly.html), [About Support Lists](https://learn.avepoint.com/fly-support-lists/about-support-lists.html)

## Design implication

If the product goal is a **user-visible reconstruction in Google Chat**, the safest documented target is:

- Teams channels → Google Chat spaces, with threads, reactions, attachments, members, and timestamps preserved where the vendor explicitly says so. [CloudM guide](https://support.cloudm.io/hc/en-us/articles/15886942950556-Migrating-Microsoft-Teams-to-Google-Workspace-Spaces), [Cloudiway guide](https://cloudiway.com/documentation/microsoft-teams-to-google-spaces-migration/), [Import data](https://developers.google.com/workspace/chat/import-data)
- Teams 1:1 chats → either a two-member Chat space/group conversation, or an archive-only representation if the chosen vendor documents email-only output. [Import data](https://developers.google.com/workspace/chat/import-data), [CloudM watchpoints](https://support.cloudm.io/hc/en-us/articles/16345799645852-Microsoft-365-to-Google-Workspace-Migration-Watchpoints)
- Teams meeting recordings, tabs, connectors, apps/bots, Planner, and Wiki should be treated as **archive/file migration problems**, not Chat reconstruction problems, because the public vendor docs either exclude them or do not document Chat equivalents. [Cloudiway guide](https://cloudiway.com/documentation/microsoft-teams-to-google-spaces-migration/), [CloudM guide](https://support.cloudm.io/hc/en-us/articles/15886942950556-Migrating-Microsoft-Teams-to-Google-Workspace-Spaces), [Import data](https://developers.google.com/workspace/chat/import-data)

If the product goal is **defensible preservation**, prefer Microsoft Purview eDiscovery plus Google Vault/Chat archiving rather than relying on Chat import mode alone. Import mode is for creating visible Chat history; Vault and Chat archiving are the compliance/archival controls. [eDiscovery export](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/purview/edisc-search-export), [Vault Chat search](https://support.google.com/vault/answer/7654076?hl=en), [Archive Chat messages](https://developers.google.com/workspace/chat/chat-archiving)
